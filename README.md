# 📊 Statistical Analysis of Risk Factors of Chronic Respiratory Diseases

## 📌 Project Overview

This repository presents a **statistical analysis of the main risk factors associated with chronic respiratory diseases**, carried out using **R**.  
The project was developed as a **group assignment** within the **Business Statistics II** course at the **University of Neuchâtel**.

The analysis focuses on evaluating the impact of **air pollution (PM2.5)** and **smoking prevalence** on **mortality rates due to chronic respiratory diseases** across countries.

---

## 🗂️ Repository Contents

The repository is structured as follows:

### 📄 Data Files

- **🌫️ `PM25.csv`**  
  Contains PM2.5 concentration levels, sourced from **IQAir’s “World’s Most Polluted Countries” dataset**.  
  This variable is used as an indicator of air pollution, where higher values correspond to poorer air quality.  
  *Source: IQAir (2023)*

- **🫁 `chron-resp.csv`**  
  Provides death rates due to chronic respiratory diseases.  
  *Source: Our World in Data*

- **🚬 `smoking.csv`**  
  Includes the percentage of adults who smoke by country.  
  *Source: Our World in Data (2015)*

---

### 💻 Statistical Analysis Script

- **`R Script.r`**  
  The main R script used to:
  - Import and clean the datasets  
  - Perform descriptive statistics  
  - Conduct regression and statistical analysis  
  - Generate results used in the final report  

---

### 📈 Final Report

- **📑 `Report.pdf`**  
  The written report presenting:
  - The research question and objectives  
  - The methodology and statistical approach  
  - Empirical results  
  - Interpretation and discussion of findings regarding the impact of smoking and air quality on chronic respiratory disease mortality  

---

## 🎯 Research Objective

The main objective of this project is to **analyze the relationship between environmental and behavioral risk factors**—specifically **air pollution and smoking**—and **mortality due to chronic respiratory diseases**, using real-world data and statistical methods.
